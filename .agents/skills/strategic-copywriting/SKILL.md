# Strategic Copywriting & Editorial Craft Playbook

Words are the interface. Great copy is concise, authentic, empathetic, and relentlessly clear.

---

## 1. The Anti-Slop Writing Filter

AI models frequently default to predictable buzzwords and hollow filler. **Strictly eliminate the following AI clichés**:

| Banned AI Buzzwords | What to Write Instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| *“delve into”, “tapestry”, “testament to”* | Use direct, specific verbs: *examine, show, record, prove*. |
| *“seamless”, “game-changer”, “revolutionize”* | State the concrete benefit: *“reduces steps from 5 to 1”*, *“syncs in 200ms”*. |
| *“In today’s fast-paced world…”* | Cut the throat-clearing intro entirely. Start directly with the core message. |
| *“It is important to remember that…”* | State the fact boldly without introductory hedges. |
| *“beacon of hope”, “journey towards excellence”* | Ground statements in verified facts, real data, and human terms. |

## 2. UX Microcopy Framework

Small copy elements carry massive cognitive weight:

### A. Buttons & Call-to-Actions (CTAs)
- **Be Specific to the Outcome**:
  - ❌ *“Submit”* → ✅ *“Generate Impact Report”*
  - ❌ *“Click here”* → ✅ *“Explore Sector Analytics”*
  - ❌ *“Download”* → ✅ *“Download September 2026 PDF (11.4 MB)”*

### B. Empty States & Skeletons
- Always tell the user 3 things:
  1. What is supposed to be here.
  2. Why it is currently empty.
  3. The exact next action they can take to populate it.
  - *Example*: *“No shelter filters selected. Choose a municipality above to view active capacity and humanitarian supplies.”*

### C. Error Messages
- Never blame the user. Follow the 3-part formula:
  1. **What happened**: *“Unable to verify live convoy data.”*
  2. **Why**: *“The telemetry server is momentarily unresponsive.”*
  3. **How to fix it**: *“Your existing data remains cached. [Retry connection] or view offline snapshot.”*

## 3. Data Storytelling & Impact Report Writing

When crafting text for impact hubs, humanitarian summaries, and executive reports:

### A. The Inverted Pyramid
1. **The Hero Stat & Core Headline**: What is the single most urgent or significant number right now?
   - *Example*: *“1.9 Million People Displaced Across Southern and Central Gaza as Essential Water Infrastructure Operates at 17% Capacity.”*
2. **Contextual Narrative (2–3 sentences)**: Explain the trend and immediate operational bottleneck.
3. **Granular Breakdown**: Support the headline with categorized metric tiles (Health, Food, Sanitation, Shelter).
4. **Action / Relief Vector**: Clearly indicate where resources are deployed and what immediate interventions are needed.

### B. Clarity Over Jargon
- Spell out acronyms on first use.
- Humanize numbers: Pair abstract totals with tangible equivalents (e.g., *“32 liters per person/day (below the 50L emergency threshold)”*).
- Maintain rigorous, respectful, and objective tone.
